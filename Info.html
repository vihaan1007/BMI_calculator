Info of BMI

  • No unit or anything random will be counted as Kg
  • Other Units are converted to Kg
  • Kg is Kg
  • Only these units are counted:
    • Quinton(100 Kg)
    • Kg(1 Kg)
    • Hg(1 / 10 Kg)
    • Dag(1 / 100 Kg)
    • g(1 / 1_000 Kg)
    • dg(1 / 10_000 Kg)
    • cg(1 / 100_000 Kg)
    • mg(1 / 1_000_000 Kg)
    • ng(1 / 10_000_000 Kg)
  • Same for height only the main unit is m and no QuinTon
  • BMI point = Weight in Kg / (Height in m)2
  • Categories:
    • UnderWeight: 18.5 or less points
    • Normal: 18.5 - 24.9 points
    • OverWeight: 25 - 29.9 points
    • Obese: 30 or more points
